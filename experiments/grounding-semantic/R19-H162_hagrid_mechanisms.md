# R19-H162 hagrid Mechanism Memo

hagrid is the arena's attributed information-seeking QA subset: a generated answer must be attributable to the retrieved passages that accompany it. The flagship reads 2-draw AUROC 0.6424 there, second lowest of ten, with a seed standard deviation of 0.0001 across three draws of the identical recipe, so a composition effect on the subset AUROC is signal rather than noise - though that invariance does NOT extend to the strata inside it. This memo names three mechanisms behind the residual, prices each from the banked R19-H161 per-pair dump across both flagship draws, and maps each to a probe and a legal lane. Four further candidates were measured and are recorded as NOT evidenced. Analysis-only arm: nothing trains, nothing is selected on arena statistics.

- **Read fidelity** - all three checkpoints reproduce their banked hagrid AUROC from the dump: flagship draw 1 0.642254 against 0.6423, flagship draw 2 0.642502 against 0.6425, enriched-mix draw 1 0.707423 against 0.7074; structural fingerprint exact on all three (250 items, 537 sentences, 1,941 pairs)
- **Sample** - 250 items, 212 supported / 38 unsupported; the negative-side instrument standard error is ~0.05-0.11 depending on stratum, so every per-stratum figure below is a coarse reading and is quoted with its Hanley-McNeil standard error
- **Seed stability is a subset-level property, NOT a stratum-level one** - the subset AUROC is seed-invariant to four decimal places, but stratum AUROCs move by up to 0.19 between the two flagship draws. Every mechanism below is therefore reported with both draws, and the ranking at the end is driven by which effects replicate
- **Artifacts** - `R19-H162_hagrid_mechanisms.py` (reader + all statistics, no GPU), `R19-H162_hagrid_mechanisms.json` (every number below), this memo
- **Prior art honoured** - R17-H147's floor autopsy (errors concentrate in the ungrounded slice, lift 3.98; discourse-frame sentences lift 1.97; single-sentence responses 56% of the subset reading 0.569), R17-H149's kill (bare-assertion verification is NOT deficient, within-pair 0.8631), R13's ceiling (12 high-scoring negatives, suppression ceiling +0.1769), and R12's label ceiling (faithful oracle 0.7833, so reachable headroom is +0.1409 not +0.3576). None of these is re-derived; two are extended below

## What a hagrid item actually is

A question, a generated free-text answer, and one to eight retrieved Wikipedia-style passages of about 700 characters each. The answer is decomposed into sentences by the shipped read; each sentence is scored against every 1,500-character window of every passage, MAX over windows, then MIN over sentences.

- **Evidence pools are shallow-to-medium** - 70 items carry 1 passage, 107 carry 2-3, 73 carry 4-8; median passage 647 characters, so most passages are a single window
- **Answers are short** - 140 of 250 items (56%) are a single sentence; median answer 156 characters
- **Answers are answer-shaped, not claim-shaped** - they open with the answer's polarity ("Yes, the Chinese helped build the railroads in the United States"), with a provenance frame ("Based on the given contexts, the oldest structure in Latvia is the Church of the Holy Spirit"), or carry inline citation markers ("[4, 5, 6]") and trailing bibliography lines
- **Seven of 250 responses carry a generation artifact** - a literal "(Context )" marker, a bare "Available: http..." line, or a response that is nothing but the frame "Based on the given context ,". All seven are labelled unsupported
- **The unsupported class is mostly relation error, not topic error** - the passage names the claim's entities and numbers but does not assert the claimed relation, and the measured surface profile below confirms it. AttributionBench's negatives have the identical shape: "Yvette Lee Bowser sings the theme song for Living Single" against a passage saying the show was "created by Yvette Lee Bowser"

## Where the loss sits

Two axes carry it. Both replicate R13's finding that hagrid's loss is high-scoring negatives, not low positives. Figures are draw 1 / draw 2.

- **Misrank mass is negative-side** - the top 12 unsupported items carry 65.4% / 68.5% of the total misranked-pair mass and score +2.37 / +2.43 mean at their sinking sentence; the worst single supported item carries 1.3%
- **Discrimination falls with evidence-pool depth on both draws** - AUROC 0.7152 / 0.6748 at 1 passage, 0.6890 / 0.6277 at 2-3, 0.5096 / 0.6052 at 4-8. The four contentless responses sit mostly in the 1-passage cell and suppress it; excluding them the same three cells read 0.8577 / 0.8129, 0.6890 / 0.6277 and 0.5096 / 0.6052, a 1-versus-4+ gap of 0.348 and 0.208 against a per-stratum standard error of ~0.09
- **The size of the score lattice is the sharper cut, and it too moves with seed** - AUROC 0.6195 / 0.5654 at 4 or fewer (sentence, window) pairs, 0.7995 / 0.8184 at 5-15, 0.3783 / 0.5435 at 16 or more. The 16-plus stratum is the worst of the three on both draws; its inversion below chance is a draw-1-only reading
- **Four contentless responses carry a fifth of the entire misrank mass on both draws** - items 49, 86, 188, 196 are the string "Based on the given context ," and nothing else; all four are unsupported and all four score POSITIVE (draw-1 logits +1.41, +2.63, +2.75, +2.07). Their misrank share is 21.2% / 20.8%; all seven artifact items together carry 27.3% / 24.1%, and excluding them lifts the subset 0.6423 to 0.6814 and 0.6425 to 0.6674

## Mechanism 1 - vacuous_claim_reject

Score a response that carries no verifiable proposition - a bare provenance frame, a stray "(Context )" marker, a bibliography line - as unsupported, rather than as trivially supported because there is nothing in it to contradict.

- **Mass** - 4 of 250 responses (1.6%) are the string "Based on the given context ," and nothing else; 7 of 250 (2.8%) carry some generation artifact. ALL SEVEN are labelled unsupported
- **The failure is total and replicates** - the four contentless responses have token containment 0.000 against their own argmax window and still score +1.41, +2.63, +2.75 and +2.07 on draw 1; they carry **21.2% / 20.8%** of hagrid's entire misranked-pair mass across the two flagship draws
- **Price, stated as a ceiling** - ranking those four items at the bottom of the subset, which is what a model with the skill would do, lifts hagrid 0.6423 to **0.7182** on draw 1 and 0.6425 to **0.7167** on draw 2: **+0.0760 / +0.0742**, draw spread 0.0018. That is the ceiling of the mechanism on this sample, not an expected gain from any lane
- **Excluding all four raises the subset to 0.6851 / 0.6834**, and it is the single change that most improves hagrid's read of any considered here
- **It also drives the single-passage stratum** - excluding the four, the 1-passage stratum reads 0.8577 / 0.8129 rather than 0.7152 / 0.6748; three of the four contentless items sit in that stratum, and they are the whole of its residual
- **Probe design** - `vacuous_reject`, held-out and rule-generated. N items whose claim side is drawn from a fixed inventory of provenance frames, citation lines, bare URLs and empty markers, paired with arbitrary passages, label 0; against a matched set of bare contentful positives over the same passages. Metric is within-pair rank accuracy that the vacuous item ranks below its contentful partner, chance 0.5. A matched control of SHORT but contentful label-1 claims is required in the probe so a model that has merely learned "short implies unsupported" scores at chance on it
- **Lane candidate** - rule-based generation, zero new data and zero new licence: emit label-0 pairs whose claim side is the same fixed inventory of contentless strings, paired with evidence sampled from the existing mix, plus the short-but-contentful label-1 control at matched length. Cap the fraction so the length channel stays at chance under the standing claim-only TF-IDF probe
- **Contamination** - CLEAR. The claim side is a hand-written inventory; the evidence side is already-banked, already-gated mix rows
- **Already covered by a banked corpus** - NO. FAVA's "unverifiable" error type is the nearest analogue but tags a span inside a contentful draft, never a contentless response. The flagship's clean 685,670-row mix contains ZERO claims that are a bare provenance frame

## Mechanism 2 - source_select

Bind a claim to the one retrieved passage that supports it, and withhold credit when the best-matching passage is only topically adjacent - the analogue of binding a value to its column header rather than to any header on the page.

The vacuous items must be removed before this is read, because three of the four sit in the 1-passage stratum and inflate the gradient. With them removed the gradient is larger, not smaller, and it replicates.

- **AUROC falls hard with evidence-pool depth on both draws** - excluding the four vacuous items: **0.8577 / 0.8129 at 1 passage** (n=66, 9 negatives), 0.6890 / 0.6277 at 2-3 (n=107, 13 negatives), **0.5096 / 0.6052 at 4-8** (n=73, 12 negatives). The 1-versus-4+ gap is 0.3481 and 0.2077, two-draw mean 0.278 against a per-stratum standard error of ~0.09
- **The score lattice is the same story from the other side** - AUROC 0.6195 / 0.5654 at 4 or fewer (sentence, window) pairs, 0.7995 / 0.8184 at 5-15, 0.3783 / 0.5435 at 16 or more, with the vacuous items still in; 16-plus is the worst stratum on both draws
- **The k-truncation curve is monotone on draw 1 only** - truncating the pool to its first k retrieved passages and re-reading the banked logits gives, on the 73 items with 4 or more passages: draw 1 0.6216 / 0.5779 / 0.5150 / 0.5232 / 0.5314 / 0.5096 at k = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, and draw 2 0.5833 / 0.6134 / 0.5369 / 0.5697 / 0.6079 / 0.6052. Both draws sit below their own 1-passage read from k = 3 onward; neither curve is monotone on draw 2. Over the whole subset the same truncation is flat-to-positive (0.6120 to 0.6423 and 0.5750 to 0.6425), so extra passages are not globally harmful - only where they are numerous
- **The confidence inversion does NOT replicate and is not offered as evidence** - on 4+ pools the mean best-passage logit is higher for unsupported items than for supported ones on draw 1 (+0.781 over 64 sentences against +0.471 over 162) but correctly ordered on draw 2 (-0.129 against +0.519)
- **Probe design** - `attr_pool`, held-out synthetic. Each item is one claim with a known single supporting passage; the positive presents the gold passage plus k topical distractors, the negative presents k+1 distractors with the gold REMOVED. Distractors are drawn by BM25 from the same corpus and filtered so none entails the claim. Sweep k in {0, 1, 3, 7}; chance 0.5 at every k. The registered read is the AUROC-versus-k slope, whose null is zero - a model with the skill is flat in k
- **Lane candidate** - rule-based BM25-distractor generator using the probe's own construction on a document-disjoint split: take a gold (claim, evidence) row, retrieve k non-entailing evidences from the same corpus, present them as extra documents, and mint the gold-removed negative. MiniCheck (MIT, 14,356 rows over 6,155 distinct documents, median chunk 922 characters) is the right source because its passages are already hagrid-sized; VitaminC (CC-BY-SA-3.0) supplies volume. PubHealth (MIT) is NOT suitable as-is - its articles run to a 3,731-character median and would each become a multi-window document, changing the geometry the probe is testing
- **Contamination** - CLEAR. MiniCheck and VitaminC both hold GREEN R14-H136 8-gram Jaccard verdicts against the ten walled arena corpora; hagrid is never a source
- **Already covered by a banked corpus** - NO. AttributionBench is the closest attribution supervision available and it packs its references into ONE evidence chunk per row (median 296 characters, 1,229 rows at 3 references, 1,484 at 4), so it never presents the model with a choice among competing passages

## Mechanism 3 - overclaim_near_copy_reject

Refuse credit to a claim that restates its passage almost verbatim yet asserts something the passage does not license - the strictness half of grounding, as opposed to the matching half.

- **The over-credited negatives are MORE lexically matched than genuine supported claims, and this replicates** - excluding the four vacuous items, the ten worst-ranked unsupported items' sinking sentences carry token containment **0.783 / 0.847** against 0.723 / 0.728 for supported items, max common n-gram **8.0 / 7.8** against 6.28 / 6.19, numeral containment 1.000 / 0.833 against 0.830 / 0.831, and they score **+2.164 / +2.314** against +0.059 / -0.293
- **Correctly-rejected negatives look nothing like them** - token containment 0.516 / 0.479, max common n-gram 4.21 / 3.50, numeral containment 0.392 / 0.324, logit -3.456 / -3.960. The model's rejection behaviour tracks surface mismatch, not unsupportedness
- **The failing items are near-copies with a scope or referent defect** - item 183 ("Lawmakers renamed it as West Virginia University on December 4, 1868") is verbatim in its passage but its pronoun has no antecedent inside the response; item 43 ("The first Mac OS was developed in 1999 as Mac OS X Server 1.0") copies a true fact under a false scope; item 24 restates a passage's numbers under a changed subject
- **Overlap sensitivity is measurable and positive** - the Spearman correlation between the argmax logit and token containment is +0.475 on supported sentences (n=413, p 1.1e-24); the model's score is substantially a containment read
- **Probe design** - `nearcopy_overclaim`, held-out and rule-generated. Positives are verbatim or near-verbatim restatements of a passage sentence; negatives are the SAME restatement with one licence-breaking edit that leaves surface overlap intact - scope widening ("the first X" for "an X"), referent replacement, quantifier strengthening ("all" for "many"), or antecedent deletion. Stratify by max common n-gram so the near-copy stratum (n-gram >= 8) is read separately; within-pair accuracy, chance 0.5
- **Lane candidate** - two options, both legal. (a) VitaminC (CC-BY-SA-3.0), already in the mix and built exactly this way from contrastive Wikipedia revisions where one edit flips the verdict - the deficit here is that the mechanism is present in the mix and still not installed, so the lane would be a re-weighting, not new supply. (b) Rule-based scope/quantifier/referent corruption over already-banked prose passages on the R17-H146 minimal-pair discipline, which covers the specific edit types VitaminC's revision distribution under-samples
- **Contamination** - CLEAR. VitaminC is already in the flagship mix and gated; a rule-based corruptor runs over already-banked evidence
- **Already covered by a banked corpus** - PARTIALLY, and this is the honest weak point. VitaminC is 34.7% of the flagship's pairs and is the textbook near-miss construction, so a fourth near-miss lane needs an argument for why more of the same would work. FAVA (CC-BY-4.0, banked, 29,436 negatives against 637 positives) targets exactly this and is the competing credit for the H159 hagrid gain

## Measured but NOT evidenced as bottlenecks

Named here so they are not re-derived, and so no lane is built on them. Each was a live candidate that a matched control or the second draw killed.

- **A general answer-frame-strip skill** - the model does score wrapped sentences low (a discourse frame is the item's sinking sentence 81.8% of the time on both draws, mean argmax logit -0.130 / -0.673 against +0.811 / +0.603 for plain sentences), and dropping the 22 discourse-frame sentences from the MIN appears to buy +0.0576 / +0.0570. **The gain is entirely the four contentless items.** Dropping the frame sentence from the other 18 frame-carrying responses moves hagrid **-0.0164 / -0.0149**, which sits at the 95th and 97.5th percentile of a matched null that drops one RANDOM sentence from the same 18 items (null mean -0.019, sd 0.002-0.003, 200 draws). A frame prefix on a contentful answer costs no more than any other sentence
- **A yes/no polarity-verification deficit** - the 22 items opening "Yes,"/"No," hold 9 of the subset's 38 negatives and read AUROC 0.4274 on draw 1 but 0.6838 on draw 2; on the polarity sentence alone, 0.4359 / 0.5641. The sentence-level penalty is stable (mean logit -1.622 / -1.438, and supported answers such as "Yes, Armenia was a part of the Soviet Union" score -4.03) but the item-level cost is unmeasurable at 9 negatives. Keep it as a probe arm inside `vacuous_reject`, not as a mechanism
- **A single-passage relation-binding deficit independent of the pool** - in the 1-passage stratum the over-credited negatives have LOW token containment (0.222 / 0.291), not high; they are the contentless items again. Once those leave, the 1-passage stratum reads 0.8577 / 0.8129 and has almost no residual to explain
- **An inline-citation-marker effect** - 26 sentences carry "[N]" markers, sink their item 73.1% of the time and score -1.054 / -1.866. No item-level price was isolated and no matched control was run; recorded as unmeasured, not as absent

## Where the enriched mix's +0.0650 actually landed

The R19-H159 enriched-mix checkpoint reads hagrid 0.7074 against the flagship pair's 0.6423 / 0.6425. Its dump reproduces that to 0.000023 with the fingerprint exact, so the contrast below is a per-pair read of the same 1,941 pairs under two weight vectors. Deltas are h159d1 minus h150d1.

- **The gain is NOT the contentless responses** - excluding the four, hagrid reads 0.6851 / 0.6834 under the flagship and **0.7295** under the enriched mix, so +0.0444 of the gain survives. The four contentless responses did fall (+1.407 to +0.570, +2.634 to +0.161, +2.749 to +1.114, +2.073 to +0.227) but they are still scored POSITIVE and still carry 17.3% of the misrank mass against 21.2% / 20.8%. Mechanism 1 is untouched by the enriched mix
- **The gain is almost entirely mechanism 2** - stratum deltas: **ndoc_4plus +0.1994**, **npair_16plus +0.2956**, **nsent_4plus +0.2109**, against ndoc_2_3 **-0.0147** and npair_5_15 +0.0024. With the vacuous items excluded, the 1-passage stratum goes 0.8577 / 0.8129 to **0.8187** (flat), 2-3 goes 0.6890 / 0.6277 to 0.6743, and 4-8 goes 0.5096 / 0.6052 to **0.7090**, a lift of +0.1516 over the flagship two-draw mean. The enriched mix bought hagrid the deep-evidence-pool stratum and nothing else
- **The k-truncation curve reverses sign** - on the 4+-passage items the flagship falls as passages are added (0.6216 at k=1 to 0.5096 at k=8 on draw 1) while the enriched mix RISES (0.6557 at k=1 to **0.7090** at k=8). Under the flagship an extra retrieved passage destroys discrimination; under the enriched mix it adds discrimination. That is the `source_select` skill, demonstrated on a real checkpoint
- **The confidence inversion is repaired** - on 4+ pools the mean best-passage logit is +1.267 for supported items against +0.629 for unsupported, correctly ordered with a 0.638 gap
- **The mechanism is near-copy rejection inside the deep pool** - the enriched model raised almost every logit (+0.53 mean on supported pairs, +0.30 on unsupported) with ONE exception: **deep-pool near-copy unsupported pairs fell by 1.193** (n=28, `max_common_ngram` >= 8) while deep-pool near-copy supported pairs rose +0.232 (n=110) and deep-pool ordinary supported pairs rose +0.700. Across the whole subset, high-containment (>= 0.80) unsupported pairs fell 0.398 while their supported counterparts rose 0.202. Mechanism 3 is what mechanism 2 is made of: the flagship's MAX-over-windows finds a spurious near-verbatim match, and the more passages it has the more often it finds one
- **Shallow pools got WORSE** - on 1-3-passage items the enriched model raised unsupported pairs more than supported ones (+0.713 against +0.303), which is why ndoc_2_3 moved -0.0147. The trade is real and it is why this is a mechanism reading, not a recommendation to adopt the H159 mix (which lost 0.026 of arena mean)
- **The wrapper penalty is partly repaired as a side effect** - polarity openers move from mean logit -1.622 / -1.438 to **+0.220** and from sinking 54.5% of their items to 40.9%; inline citation markers move -1.054 / -1.866 to +0.406. Discourse frames do NOT recover (-0.130 / -0.673 to -0.392, sinking rate 81.8% unchanged), and the frame-drop diagnostic still buys +0.0366 on the enriched checkpoint against +0.0576 / +0.0570 on the flagship

## AttributionBench localisation, and the honest verdict on it

The R19-H159 verdict credits AttributionBench with this +0.0650. The pair-level signature does not confirm that credit and points at least as strongly at FAVA.

- **What the signature shows** - the gain is carried by suppression of high-overlap unsupported windows inside deep evidence pools (-1.193 logits on 28 deep-pool near-copy unsupported pairs, with supported near-copies rising). That is the exact prediction the R19-H161 arm registered for **FAVA's** overlap-prior suppression lane, whose H1 statement reads "hagrid rises because overlap is a distractor in attribution over web snippets"
- **AttributionBench predicts the same direction by a different route** - its negatives are high-overlap relation errors ("Neil Young sings 'Only Love Can Break Your Heart'" against a reference saying he WROTE it), so it also teaches near-copy rejection. It is 16,444 rows at 35.2% negatives; FAVA is 30,073 rows at 97.9% negatives. Both are live
- **The register-match discriminator is inconclusive** - AttributionBench's claims are short (median 105 characters) and its evidence chunks short (median 296); FAVA's claims are long drafts (mean 606) over long chunks (mean 2,972). If either corpus's register drove the gain, hagrid's gain should concentrate at the matching claim and window lengths. It does not: the largest supported-minus-unsupported separation sits at 500-1,000-character windows (+0.475), which is hagrid's own passage size and neither corpus's. Claim-length bins separate +0.321 (< 120 chars), +0.222 (120-250) and -0.610 (>= 250, n=86, noise)
- **What this does NOT establish** - a signature match is not an ablation. Five corpora entered together and this contrast has one enriched draw. The registered credit for AttributionBench stands as recorded; this memo records that the pair-level evidence is equally consistent with FAVA and that the question is open
- **AttributionBench's structural limit, measured** - whatever it taught, it did not teach pool selection directly: every AttributionBench row packs its references into ONE evidence chunk (median 296 characters, 1,229 rows at 3 references, 1,484 at 4), so the model never sees competing passages during training on it. If the deep-pool gain is AttributionBench's, it arrived by transfer from single-chunk relation rejection, not by direct supervision

## Isolation test

The R19-H159 verdict credits AttributionBench with hagrid's +0.0650, but the same arm's own H1 lane credits FAVA's overlap-prior suppression with it, and both corpora entered together. Two claims must be separated: "AttributionBench taught attribution" versus "something else in that mix happened to help hagrid".

- **What a 2-arm AttributionBench-alone test would be expected to show** - the lane is 16,444 rows on 721,210 (+2.3% rows, +1.3% pairs), so it de-weights every existing lane by 0.9875 against the H159 mix's 0.8248. The table-subset collapse that killed H159 (finqa -0.1429, tatqa -0.1328) should NOT reproduce at that dilution, and the arena mean should land within noise of the flagship's 0.71549. On hagrid the honest expectation is a gain smaller than +0.0650, because the H159 arm confounds AttributionBench's own contribution with the removal of 17.5% of pair mass from the table lanes; a plausible band is +0.02 to +0.05
- **hagrid is the instrument that makes one draw enough** - three draws of the identical recipe read 0.6423, 0.6425, 0.6424, a standard deviation of 0.0001, against 0.007 to 0.037 for every other subset. Any hagrid move above roughly 0.005 in a single draw is signal. That is an n=3 observation of one recipe, not a law, but it is the strongest per-subset instrument in the arena
- **The cheapest measurement is not a training run at all** - build the three probes above (`vacuous_reject`, `attr_pool`, `nearcopy_overclaim`, all held-out and rule-generated, no arena data) and score the THREE ALREADY-BANKED checkpoints h150d1, h150d2 and h159d1 on them. Cost is one scoring pass, roughly 0.2 GPU-h, zero training. The reading separates the credits directly: a win on `attr_pool` (distractor rejection over competing passages) is AttributionBench's attribution skill, because AttributionBench is the only new corpus in the answer-with-references shape; a win on `nearcopy_overclaim`, whose near-copy stratum is exactly what overlap-prior suppression targets, is FAVA's; a win on `vacuous_reject` alone is neither, and points at the enriched mix's negative-class prior
- **The free discriminator has already been read** - the gain survives excluding the four contentless responses (0.6851 / 0.6834 to 0.7295, +0.0444), so it is a skill gain and not a lowered score on contentless text. That rules out the cheapest null explanation but does not separate the two corpora
- **What would NOT settle it** - a leave-one-out over all six R19 corpora (5 trainings) buys a full attribution table but costs 25-30 GPU-h and is not warranted until the probe read is in. Nor would a 2-arm AttributionBench-alone test on its own: a positive result would be consistent with either corpus, since FAVA is not in that arm to be ruled out. The 2-arm test answers 'is AttributionBench sufficient', the probes answer 'which skill moved'

## Ranking

Expected arena movement per unit of build cost. The enriched-mix contrast reorders this against what the flagship draws alone would have suggested.

1. **`source_select`** - the enriched-mix checkpoint is a live demonstration that this skill is learnable and that installing it is worth **+0.1516** on hagrid's deep-pool stratum and +0.0444 on the subset with the vacuous items held out. The k-truncation curve flips from falling to rising, which is as clean a mechanism confirmation as this campaign has produced on hagrid. Build cost is the highest of the three (a BM25-distractor generator over MiniCheck and VitaminC plus a k-sweep probe), but it is the only mechanism with a checkpoint-level existence proof
2. **`overclaim_near_copy_reject`** - the same evidence promotes this too, because the deep-pool gain IS near-copy suppression (-1.193 logits on deep-pool near-copy unsupported pairs). It is ranked second rather than first only because two banked corpora already target it (VitaminC at 34.7% of the flagship's pairs, FAVA banked and unused), so the first question is a re-weighting question, not a new-supply question. It is also the mechanism that cost H159 its table subsets, since on finqa and tatqa high overlap IS the honest cue - any lane here must carry a table-register control
3. **`vacuous_claim_reject`** - the cheapest build by an order of magnitude (a hand-written inventory of contentless claim strings against evidence already in the mix, hours of work, no new corpus or licence) and the only mechanism the enriched mix did NOT fix: the four contentless responses still score positive and still carry 17.3% of hagrid's misrank mass. Its ceiling on this sample is +0.0760 / +0.0742 and it replicates to 0.0018. It is ranked third on expected arena movement, because four items in one of ten subsets is at most +0.008 of arena mean, and first on cost-effectiveness

**Build first: `vacuous_claim_reject`** - not because it is the largest, but because it is the only one that can be built and probed this week with no new data, no licence question and no contamination surface, and because the enriched-mix contrast proves the other two are already partially addressed by supply the campaign has banked. Register `attr_pool` and `nearcopy_overclaim` as probes in the same wave and score the three banked checkpoints on them; that read, not a training arm, decides whether either earns a lane.

## Not proposed, and why

- **Aggregation redesign** - R17-H147 measured 8 re-aggregations of banked per-sentence scores and found the shipped MIN best or within 0.03 everywhere, with argmin localisation 0.636-0.727. Nothing here reopens that; every diagnostic drop in this memo is a measurement instrument, and every fix proposed is a training lane
- **Retrieval geometry** - R17-H147 measured multi-doc-evidence lift 0.49/1.15 and window-overflow lift 0.53/0.46, both anti-concentrated. Mechanism 2 is not a retrieval-geometry proposal; it is a training-data proposal about distractor rejection, and it is priced on the banked logits rather than on a re-retrieval
- **Partial-support strictness as a standalone lane** - R13-H128 built exactly this from WiCE and moved hagrid -0.0023 against a +0.02 bar. The post-mortem attributes the null to the build keeping partially-supported claims as label-1 positives, so the mechanism is not refuted, but mechanism 3 above inherits that failure and is ranked last because of it
- **A serving-side sentence filter** - dropping wrapper or artifact sentences from the MIN at serve time would post the diagnostic numbers in this memo directly onto the arena. It is not proposed: the diagnostic is a measurement of where the loss sits, and turning it into a rule selected on arena statistics is exactly what the campaign's discipline forbids
- **Label noise as a lever** - item 183 ("Lawmakers renamed it as West Virginia University on December 4, 1868", labelled unsupported) is verbatim in its passage; what fails is that the pronoun "it" has no antecedent inside the response. R12's banked faithful-oracle ceiling of 0.7833 already prices this class. Reachable headroom is +0.1409, not the raw +0.3576

## Instrument limits

- **38 negatives in 250** - per-stratum negative counts run 3 to 13, so stratum AUROCs carry standard errors of 0.06 to 0.13. Every stratum claim above is directional evidence, not a resolved fact
- **hagrid's famous seed invariance does not extend below the subset level** - the subset AUROC differs by 0.0002 between the two flagship draws, but ndoc_4plus moves 0.0956, npair_16plus moves 0.1652 and nsent_4plus moves 0.1909 between the same two draws. Any stratum-level claim in this memo needs both draws quoted, and the pool-depth mechanism in particular is named on the ordering, not on a number
- **The pool-depth and lattice-size axes are correlated** - deep pools produce many (sentence, window) pairs by construction, so mechanism 2's two readings are one effect seen twice, not two effects
- **The k-doc truncation reads banked logits under a modified pool** - it does not re-score, so it measures how the AGGREGATOR behaves as the pool grows, not how a model trained on shallow pools would behave
- **Surface features are regex-defined** - the wrapper, artifact and citation classes come from patterns fitted by reading the subset; a different pattern set would move the counts by a few sentences. The four frame-only responses are exact string matches and are not sensitive to this
