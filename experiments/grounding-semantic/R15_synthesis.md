## 0. What repairs the defect - the three sentences

**The defect is neither arithmetic nor capacity: the pretrained trunk already separates magnitude at R² 0.9987 and ordering at held-out accuracy 1.000 from 200 examples (P4 Instrument B, three controls holding), while the task head reads every combinatorial derivation at chance (0.484-0.526 across ten types on 5,008 held-out quads, P1 §3) and resolves a row label at 0.9936 but a column header at 0.5179 on byte-identical templates (L1 §0, replicated 0.9943 / 0.5276 on the admitted H108 lane) - the head has exactly one numeric competence, digit copying, and no supervision anywhere in 746,854 training rows that makes it compose a relation over two cells.** What repairs it is supervision, in two independent and complementary pieces that must not be run in one arm: R14-H133's lane rebuilt to the corrected spec - eight operations instead of one, coarse-first negatives, byte-identical pair templates, a six-body-row evidence budget, N7 capped at 10% - which teaches **ordering inside** the absent-number region, and a zero-build re-weighting of the 87,933 absent-positive rows that already exist and are already labelled, which moves the pooled shortcut P(label 0 | absent) from 0.6433 to exactly 0.5000 and fixes the region's **level**, where the 50,000-row lane alone moves it only to 0.6192. **No objective term in this field can invent the missing supervision and none is proposed to, so the three cheapest items on the board are all measurements: 0.05 GPU-h settles whether the defect is recipe-wide or one checkpoint's and it blocks the lane's build spec, 0.25 GPU-h settles whether the model conditions on evidence numerals at all outside the verbatim-copy route, and 0.15 GPU-h settles whether the header-binding hole survives a digit-length control - and either of the last two can re-scope the round.**

**Total first-decision cost for the whole round: ~2.0 GPU-h, every read frozen-weights, held-out, arena-free, on cards 0 or 2. Card 1 is untouched.**

### R15-B4 - RELATIONAL SUB-BLOCK: cross-header binding and ordering (**NEW: R15-H138**, sub-block form recommended)
*Source: L1-C3 (LANE lens), with P4 §6's learnability ranking.*
