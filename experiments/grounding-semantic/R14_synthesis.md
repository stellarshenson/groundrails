## 0. The two verdicts, first

### A. Why finqa collapsed under the margin arm (-0.1020, paired seed 1117)

**The margin arm trained 13,898 of its 30,369 lane rows (45.8%) with no absolute target at all** - verified from source in this session: `DR_lane_assemble.py` writes every clean partner as `label=-1, bce_mask=True`, and `DR_lane_trainer.py:17` states the clean partners "DO enter the domain loss; they never enter BCE" - so the only anchored gradient those pairs contributed was a one-sided downward push on the *corrupt* member, whose edits are 7,862 number-changes and 13,314 entity-swaps (`DR_judge_summary.json`, verified). **finqa is the arena's extreme claim-side numeric register** - claim digit fraction 0.0997 against an arena median near 0.013, 5.78 numeric tokens per sentence (E1) - and the shipped model already penalises numeric claims *against* the label (sentence score 0.6765 at zero numeric tokens falling to 0.518 at 6+, a bucket that is 95.4% faithful), so an unanchored downward pressure on number-edited claims deepens an existing defect exactly where finqa's faithful majority lives, and at 20 negatives with median rank 62.5 two rank swaps are worth 0.10. **The window-multiplicity account E1 offers for the same collapse is not supported by its own zero-GPU precursor, which I ran here** [JUDGE]: across the ten subsets, windows-per-sentence correlates with the margin delta at Pearson **-0.113** (Spearman -0.576, p = 0.082), and techqa - at **22.5** windows per sentence, four times finqa's 5.18 - moved **+0.0034** while finqa moved **-0.1020**.

Recorded alongside: only two subsets clear the noise floor on this paired contrast (emanual +0.1561 at z = +7.4, finqa -0.1020 at z = -2.4, E3); the delucionqa -0.0392 is z = -0.91 and adjudicates nothing. `gold_full` saw none of it (margin 0.8042 vs control 0.8040) - the damage was invisible to every in-domain instrument the arm carried.

### B. Why delucionqa is read-geometry-sensitive

**delucionqa's window exposure is class-asymmetric by construction of RAGBench's retrieval, not by anything the model does** - recomputed here from the gate-A parquet [JUDGE]: 130 of 172 grounded responses (**75.58%**) sit on at least one multi-window document against 4 of 12 ungrounded ones (**33.33%**), 8 of the 12 negatives sit in the single-window bucket that windowing cannot move at all, and 218 of 552 documents (**39.49%**) exceed the 1,500-char window. **Every geometry change that adds premise coverage therefore hands almost all of its lift to the positive class** - the counterfactual decomposition prices it at +0.0959 on positives against -0.0489 given back on negatives (E2 §4.2) - which is why windowing is 10/10 sign-positive on this subset at mean +0.0555 while no training lane has ever moved it outside the clean recipe's own seed range. **The sensitivity is a property of the label-and-retrieval geometry, not of grounding capability, and it is not adjudicable**: 85% of delucionqa's config-to-config variance is seed noise, its seed sigma 0.0432 is 89% of the analytic AUROC standard error on 12 negatives, zero of fourteen trained configurations move it beyond 2 sigma, and every banked read already sits above its own faithful-oracle ceiling of 0.6657 - so a delucionqa gain measures readier partial-support firing, not better grounding.

**Binding consequence for the register**: delucionqa is a diagnostic surface in R14 and a no-collapse hold clause. It is barred from adjudicating any hypothesis. Every block below honours this.
